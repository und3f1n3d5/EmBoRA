## C2 — Structural activation separated from finite-frame effects

Question: does a specified path admit a legal-state intervention changing its designated observable? For UG responder C3 separates three observables: raw candidate targets, value-sensitive E proposal, and final selected action. Required structural evidence is (i) raw q invariance under priority/state/impulse changes, (ii) at the same offer/state, material_high and relationship_high accept while fairness_high rejects in the F-35 extreme-offer fixture, and (iii) neutral-hybrid R selection may mask that E-proposal difference. Other paths retain S03/AT-THRESHOLD/AT-SUPPRESSION. A legal injected state is not automatically reachable from neutral initialization. Label `analytic_existence`, `legal_state_fixture`, and, only if demonstrated later, `neutral_reachable` separately.

Analytic scope: F-35's channel ordering is invariant to current values and other current state once offer, priorities and adapter are fixed. Under clipped priorities N/B normative rejection is tie-boundary-only; A always accepts; N/B proposal partitions are identical on the legal grid. Material and responder safety vote in the same payoff direction but are separately weighted channels, not `pbar` squared. `material_only` leaves all four candidate votes in place and changes only realized applied targets. State/history can still change F-14 branch selection and later trajectories. These restrictions are mandatory disclosures, not findings.

Falsifier: raw q changes with priorities/current values/impulse; F-35 weights a vote more than once; candidate projections mutate state; exact replay does not produce the declared profile-specific proposal/final-action partition; all-state/N-B-equivalence oracles fail; or divergence arises from leakage/multiple interventions. Proposal responsiveness remains distinct from final-action responsiveness: neutral hybrid UG is a required final-action null. BoS cannot furnish behavioral arbitration witnesses. C2a alone does not support prevalence or general relevance.

### C2b: representative-within-frame candidate

Question: in **DF-C3-NEUTRAL-200**, how frequently and by how much does each focal switch change behavior relative to paired full? “Representative” means an equally weighted census of the explicitly chosen finite factors/opponents, with no assertion that these weights describe a natural population. All episodes start neutrally; no conditioning on future divergence. C3 changes the UG-R mechanism, so previous design arithmetic is retained but no C2 trajectory/result is carried forward.

Primary mechanisms are no_mood,no_fatigue,fixed_threshold,material_only. no_suppression is secondary C4. For each eligible stratum g∈{PD,UG-P,UG-R}, switch z, condition c, define:

- τ_A(c,z)=min round with different final focal action; T+1 if none (right-censored at horizon, never omitted).
- I_A(c,z)=1[τ_A≤T]. Primary prevalence P_A(g,z)=mean_c I_A over **all** conditions in that stratum.
- D_A(c,z)=T^−1 Σ_t 1[a_full(t)≠a_z(t)]. Action equality uses canonical objects including offer amount. Report offer-distance T^−1Σ|o_full−o_z|/(o_max−o_min) additionally for UG-P; it does not replace D_A.
- ΔP(c,z)=mean_t π_full(t)−mean_t π_z(t); ΔS(c,z)=mean_t S_full(t)−mean_t S_z(t). Positive ΔP favors full only for this operational payoff comparison. Positive ΔS is not human wellbeing.
- τ_proposal,τ_threshold,τ_state are analogous diagnostic first differences (continuous differences use YAML output tolerance). They never substitute for τ_A in behavioral prevalence.

Report per stratum/switch: cell count, P_A, full τ_A survival/first-divergence frequency with no-divergence mass, D_A and ΔP/ΔS distributions (mean, median, empirical 10th/90th percentiles, min/max), and proportion of positive/zero/negative effects. Per-condition data remain available. Quantile rule is nearest-rank: kth ordered value with k=max(1,ceil(pN)); median is middle value for odd N and arithmetic mean of middle two for even N. No “significant effect” language for a deterministic census.

Identifiability diagnostics: report rate of E/R proposal disagreement, selected branch, F-35 `N_VS`-derived categories `{positive_accept,negative_reject,tie_reject}`, normalized `D_VS` as a separate scalar diagnostic, and action differences with/without prior disagreement. Never infer the category from D. Report priority profiles and selected adapter separately; a value-sensitive proposal difference hidden by R selection is not a final-action effect. N/B proposal equivalence cannot support robustness of their different q magnitudes. Conditioning on full-run disagreement is a labelled descriptive subset, never the primary denominator or a causal subgroup estimand. No exclusion of saturated or low-impulse conditions. Opponent's response to changed history is part of total simulator effect; no natural mediation claim is made.

Falsification/narrowing: P_A=0 across a stratum/switch supports a null behavioral result there even if internal states differ. Rare activation does not become general relevance. Adverse/mixed ΔP/ΔS are preserved. If effects exist only in injected witnesses, report C2a and do not promote C2b. Absence in this finite frame does not prove impossibility in all legal states. Allowed wording names exact game/role, switch, frame, prevalence and effect; no human causality or universal benefit.

## C3 — Preregistered operational ranking divergence

Candidate: in the frozen comparison roster, payoff and the specified internal-state metric may rank paired trajectories differently. This is an operational metric question; neither its existence nor its prevalence is assumed. The normative adapter is fixed explicitly as `normative_resisted_inequity_v1`; A/B are sensitivity-only. Candidate and realized vectors must come from the same selected adapter; implicit/default selection or mixed adapters invalidate the run.

Primary comparison set: full hybrid versus each of all five single-switch variants in each B02 condition; report g∈{PD,BoS,UG-P,UG-R} separately and z separately. Do not create comparisons between unrelated histories or choose extremal trajectories after seeing results. Kind controls are descriptive secondary; stateless baselines have no S and cannot enter C3.

Primary metrics: P=arithmetic mean of focal payoff over rounds 1…T and Q=arithmetic mean of **post-round** S over the same rounds. S is the behavioral reference state F-27 with unit lambdas; initial S0 is not added as an extra observation. Normalize ΔP by the focal maximum payoff for that game/role (PD5,BoS3,UG8, derived from S02) and ΔQ by the reference S range width (4). For either normalized contrast x, define rank sign r(x)=−1 if x<−10^−9, +1 if x>10^−9, otherwise 0. Equality at ±10^−9 is a tie. This ranking threshold is a metric definition and never changes model decision branches.

For each pair, discordance J=1[r(ΔP)r(ΔQ)=−1]. Primary discordance prevalence D(g,z)=mean_c J over **all** B02 conditions in that stratum, including ties. Also report concordance, both-tied, payoff-only-tied and state-only-tied fractions (exhaustive disjoint categories). Conditional discordance among strict non-tied comparisons is secondary and must show its denominator. Pairwise sign disagreement is not correlation and not Pareto optimality.

Counterexamples are diagnostic only: equal-payoff trajectories with different S are payoff ties, not strict discordance. They may be reported under a separate `payoff_tied_state_different` category. A constructed state or selected metric weight cannot establish D>0 in the neutral finite frame. No “payoff optimization is wrong,” “psychological wellbeing improved,” or universal trade-off claim.

Falsifier/limitation: zero strict discordance in the frame; apparent discordance caused by inconsistent initialization, incorrect q, missing cells or unequal comparison units; divergence that is confined to one fragile convention/weighting must be described as such. It is permissible for C3 to be null or too sensitive for a central finding.

## C5 — Units, exclusions, multiplicity, protocol and promotion

<a id="c5-u"></a>

### C5-U — Units and uncertainty

Primary B02 is a deterministic finite-frame census. No standard errors, confidence intervals, bootstrap sampling intervals or p-values are attached to its cell means/frequencies as if seeds or rounds were sampled. Variation across designed conditions is heterogeneity, not estimated sampling error. A one-per-condition finite count remains the count even if trajectories are identical.

If a post-U1/U2-approved extension samples conditions or uses stochastic opponents, the protocol must name the target probability distribution, independent sampling/RNG cluster, event-indexed stream construction and pair key. Pair full/z within each independent cluster; treat repeated rounds, role-swaps using shared randomness, and multiple switches sharing a full trajectory as dependent. Independent streams may produce identical trajectories; retain both valid draws. Distinct hashes may share one latent draw; retain their cluster dependence. Unknown randomness provenance prohibits inferential claims, rather than being repaired by deduplication.

The prospective protocol must choose estimator/interval methods compatible with its actual design, preregister precision targets and sample size *before* pilot interpretation, and distinguish stochastic-population uncertainty from deterministic frame heterogeneity. For a simple independent paired-draw design, use intervals at the independent-pair cluster, never the round; if finite-frame sampling without replacement is used, include its sampling design/correction. No unspecified stochastic evidence is licensed now.

<a id="c5-e"></a>

### C5-E — Exclusions and failure accounting

No valid trajectory is excluded for agreement, no divergence, low payoff, rejection, saturation, hash duplication or adverse effect. Invalid schema/actions, broken event counters, missing traces, code/spec mismatch, incomplete episodes or undeclared environment changes cause **paired-cell invalidation**: preserve both sides and failure reason, report missing count, and block complete-frame claim promotion until corrected reruns are frozen under a new run ID. No silent imputation or replacement. Technical rerun selection uses a predeclared repair rule and preserves failed originals.

BoS behavioral arbitration is `not_identifiable` by design, not a failed run. UG-P/UG-R strata and stateless comparator S omissions are planned not-applicable fields, not outcome-based exclusions. Late-start/warmup deletion is forbidden in primary summaries; last-half means are sensitivity only.
